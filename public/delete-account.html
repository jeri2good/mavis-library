Mavis Library

Delete your account

For the Mavis Library website and the Mavis Library Android app.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Mavis Library and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings, then Account (Account is also in the main menu).
  3. Open Delete account, choose "Delete my account and everything synced to it", enter your password, and confirm.

Forgot your password? Use "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in screen with the recovery code you saved when you created the account, then delete the account.

What gets deleted

  • Your email address and your password and recovery code hashes.
  • Everything synced to the account: shelf, reading progress, notes, highlights and quotes, reading plans, and word builder vocabulary.

Deletion is immediate and can't be undone.

What is not deleted automatically

  • Book clubs and study groups. Leave each group before deleting your account; that removes your membership, display name and reading position. Posts you wrote stay in the group, so delete any you want gone first.
  • Copies on your devices. Books, audio and notes saved on a phone, tablet or computer stay there until you sign out with "Also remove this account's shelf, notes, and downloaded books from this device", clear the site's data, or uninstall the app.

Can't sign in?

If you can't sign in and don't have your recovery code, email Jermaine.hawthorne@gmail.com from the address you used for the account and ask for it to be deleted.

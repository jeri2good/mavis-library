Mavis Library

Privacy policy

Applies to the Mavis Library website (mavis-library.netlify.app) and the Mavis Library Android app. Both run the same code.

The short version

  • You can use Mavis Library without an account. Then your books, reading progress and notes stay on your device.
  • If you create an account, the server keeps your email address, a salted hash of your password, and the reading data you sync.
  • Everything sent between the app and the server travels over HTTPS (encrypted in transit).
  • You can delete your account and its synced data at any time, in the app or as described on the delete account page.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.

What stays on your device

Book files you download or import, saved audiobook audio, settings, the owner access code (if entered) and the kids mode PIN (stored as a salted hash) are kept in your browser or app storage on this device. They are not uploaded. Removing the app or clearing site data removes them.

Accounts and sync

Accounts are optional. When you create one, Mavis stores on its server (Netlify Blobs):

  • your email address;
  • your password and recovery code as salted hashes (scrypt), never the plain text;
  • the data you sync: your shelf (book details, not the book files), reading progress, notes, highlights and quotes, reading plans, and word builder vocabulary.

Book clubs and Bible study groups store the group name, the display name you choose, your reading position in the group's book or plan, and the posts you write. Other members see your display name, position and posts, never your email address.

Optional services the site owner can switch on instead of, or as well as, the ones above: Anthropic (AI text), Google Cloud Text-to-Speech (cloud read-aloud), and TypeSafe AI or Eden AI (ranking "Picked for you" suggestions, which receives the genres you like and the titles you read recently).

Voice search and asking Mavis by voice use your device's speech recognition. Your browser or phone maker may process that audio under its own policy. Microphone audio is only used for voice features and is only recorded when you start one.

Reports about AI content

If you use "Report this" on an AI answer, picture, summary or scene film, Mavis stores a short note on its server (Netlify Blobs): what kind of content it was, which book or passage, any text you add, the time and the app version. It is not linked to your account. The site owner reads these notes to fix problems.

Kids mode

Kids mode is a setting a parent turns on with a PIN on one device. It hides grown-up books and asks the AI to keep answers child-friendly. It does not create a separate account; AI requests only carry an extra flag asking for child-safe answers. Mavis Library is meant for people aged 13 and over; children should use it with a parent.

Deleting your data

In the app: open Settings, then Account, then Delete account, and confirm with your password. This permanently removes your email address, password hash, and every synced shelf, progress, note, plan and vocabulary record from the server. Book club and study group records are not removed automatically: leave each group first (this removes your membership, display name and reading position) and delete any posts you want gone, because posts stay in the group. Copies on your own devices stay until you sign out with "also remove from this device" or clear the app's data. See the delete account page if you can't sign in.
